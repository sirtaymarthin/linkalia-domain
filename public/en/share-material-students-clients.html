What goes wrong when you send material piecemeal

  • Each file arrives through a different channel and gets lost between messages.
  • When you update a PDF, the old and new versions live side by side.
  • Whoever joins mid-course doesn't have what came before.
  • Drive folders ask for accounts and permissions and aren't great on a phone.

A Channel: you publish, they're always up to date

In Linkalia, a collection shared as a Channel works like this: only you edit, and followers see it on their phone, sorted into categories and always current. Whenever you add something, it reaches them straight away.

Some examples that come with the app:

  • Academy or exam prep: PDF notes, explainer videos and past papers by topic.
  • Driving school: practice tests, manoeuvre videos and documents for the theory test.
  • Coach or running club: training plans, Strava or AllTrails routes and technique videos.
  • Estate agent: property sheets, photos, floor plans and the Maps location for each client.
  • Online course: lessons, readings and resources for each module.

How to create your Channel

  1. Create a collection and set up categories (“Unit 1”, “Videos”, “Past papers”).
  2. Add material from any app with Share: PDFs, YouTube videos, Word documents, photos, links.
  3. Tap its name to open Collection info and, under Access, pick Channel.
  4. In the ⋮ menu, tap Share invite link: send the link or put the QR code on the screen in class. Anyone without the app lands on the page to download it.

You decide who follows the Channel: you can see the list of followers and remove anyone who should no longer have access.

What about people who don't want to install anything?

With Pro you can export a whole collection as a web page (HTML) that opens in any browser, search included. On the free plan you can export single categories of your private collections that way.

Tip: pin this week's material to the top. That way, the first thing they see when they open the Channel is what matters.

Frequently asked questions

Can my students change the material?

No. In a Channel only the owner edits; followers can only view. If you want everyone to add things, use a Collaborative collection.

How many followers can a Channel have?

The free plan allows a small group of followers per Channel; Pro allows many more, with more space for documents.

Do they need to create an account?

No. They install Linkalia, open your link or scan the QR code, and they're in. No sign-up, no email.
