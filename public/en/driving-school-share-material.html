How to give your driving school students theory test material on their phones

Updated 4 November 2026 · Written by the Linkalia team

Your students revise for the theory test on their phones, on the bus or on a break at work. But the road signs summary is in a PDF you sent a month ago, the tests on one website, the videos on another, and the licensing paperwork nowhere. Here's how to give them everything in one link.

Everything a learner needs, in one place

  • Theory: the road signs summary, the most common offences and penalties, right-of-way rules.
  • Tests: exam-style tests as PDFs and links to official practice tests.
  • Videos: manoeuvres, roundabouts, merging and the typical mistakes in the practical test.
  • Paperwork: the licensing authority's website, booking the test, what to bring.
  • Locations: the test centre and test routes on Google Maps.

The most used items — say, the road signs infographic or the most common test mistakes — get pinned to the top.

Why it beats sending PDFs on WhatsApp

  • Each new student gets the link or scans the QR code at reception and has everything from day one.
  • When something changes (a rule, a new test), you update it once and it reaches everyone.
  • It's your material, under your school's name: you're not sending them to someone else's app.

Channel or Collaborative: which to use

Channel

one person publishes, the rest view

The driving school's Channel

  • You publish the material; students can only view it and can't change it.
  • One Channel per licence type or per intake.
  • When a student passes, you can remove them to keep the list tidy.

Collaborative

everyone adds and edits

A Collaborative collection for instructors

  • The school's instructors share material, videos and lesson sheets with each other.
  • The good stuff one finds then goes into the students' Channel.

How to set it up in five minutes

  1. Install Linkalia and create a collection named after the group.
  2. Create the categories. For example: Road signs, Tests, Videos, Paperwork, Locations.
  3. Add material with Share from any app: PDFs, Word files, photos, YouTube videos, links or Google Maps locations.
  4. Tap the collection's name to open Collection info and, under Access, pick Channel or Collaborative.
  5. In the ⋮ menu, tap Share invite link: send it to the WhatsApp group or show the QR code.

Anyone without the app lands on the page to download it for free; no sign-up or email needed. As admin you see who's in the collection and can remove (and readmit) anyone.

See it in the app: Linkalia comes with the example collection “Driving School — Theory Test 2026”. Once installed it's in your library, so you can see how it looks before creating your own.
