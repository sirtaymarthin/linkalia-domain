Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Linkalia, we take the privacy of our users very seriously. This policy clearly explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Linkalia is designed from the ground up to collect as little data as possible. It does not require registration, you do not need to provide your name or email address, and we do not display advertising.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the developer of Linkalia. You can contact us at any time at info@linkalia.org.

2. What data we collect

Anonymous device identifier
When you open the application for the first time, a unique random identifier (UUID) is automatically generated on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any personal data. It is used solely to manage your collections and your storage quota on the server.

Username (optional)
You may choose a visible name to identify yourself in collaborative collections. This name is entirely free — you can use a pseudonym or any text you prefer. We do not verify it or link it to any real identity.

Content you provide
The links, documents, images, and files you save in Linkalia are stored on servers located in the European Union (Cloudflare R2, EU jurisdiction). This content belongs to you and you control it at all times.

Subscription data
If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is handled entirely through Google Play. Linkalia only receives confirmation that the subscription is valid — we do not have access to your credit card, payment data, or billing information.

3. What data we do NOT collect

  • Real name, email address, or phone number
  • Geographic location
  • Browsing data or usage history
  • Device contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any form of cross-app tracking

4. How we use your data

  • To store and synchronise your collections between devices and collaborators
  • To manage your account's storage quota
  • To verify the status of your Premium subscription

We do not use your data for advertising, nor do we share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

5. Where data is stored

All content you upload to collaborative collections or Spaces is stored on Cloudflare servers under European Union jurisdiction. Cloudflare acts as a data processor and holds the relevant security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2). You can review their privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Personal collections that you do not share are stored exclusively on your device and are not sent to any server.

6. How long we retain data

Your content remains on our servers for as long as you have the application installed and active. If you delete a collaborative collection, the content is permanently and immediately removed from our servers. If you wish to delete all your data, please write to us at info@linkalia.org providing your account identifier (available in Settings within the app) and we will delete it within a maximum of 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access: know what data we hold about you
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a readable format
  • Objection: object to certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at info@linkalia.org. We will respond within a maximum of 30 days.

8. Security

All communication between the application and our servers uses HTTPS with TLS encryption. Collection access tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes and never in plain text. Your anonymous account identifier is generated locally and is not transmitted in any way that could identify you.

9. Minors

Linkalia is not directed at children under the age of 16. If you are under 16, please do not use the application without the consent of your legal guardian. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor without appropriate consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will notify you within the app itself. The date of the last update always appears at the top of this document. Continued use of the application after changes have been made implies acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints relating to the privacy of your data, you can write to us at:

info@linkalia.org

You may also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at aepd.es if you believe the processing of your data does not comply with applicable regulations.
